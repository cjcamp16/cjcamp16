### Projects

- **[SMOKE](https://github.com/smoke-nba-research/SMOKE)** (Shots Made Over Known Expectation): a transparent, validated measure of NBA shot-making. It grew out of the [four-person capstone](https://github.com/cjcamp16/nba-shot-quality-capstone) I led at ASU; the follow-on research models 128,069 tracked shots, cross-fits every expectation, ships confidence intervals with every value, and passes an eight-test validation battery with every success criterion written down in advance (year-over-year stability 0.48 against 0.43 for effective field goal percentage). Abstract submitted to the 2027 MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference (September 2026), co-authored with two capstone teammates.
- **[OfferReady](https://offerreadynow.us)**: an AI career kit I designed, built, and shipped end to end. Phone photos become studio-quality headshots, paired with an ATS resume rewrite, a LinkedIn overhaul, and a hosted portfolio. 12 paid orders in its first two weeks; my first project taken from an idea to a paying product.
- **[generative-content-studio](https://github.com/cjcamp16/generative-content-studio)**: one prompt becomes a story, scene art, character portraits, and a soundtrack. Five architectures (GPT-2, Stable Diffusion, StyleGAN2, LSTM, CNN style transfer) coordinated behind a single interface.
- **[sciviz-paraview](https://github.com/cjcamp16/sciviz-paraview)**: scripted scientific visualization in ParaView. NumPy to VTK 3D fields, volume rendering, isosurfaces, and streamlines.
- **[psid-income-wealth-analysis](https://github.com/cjcamp16/psid-income-wealth-analysis)**: panel-data study of U.S. income and wealth inequality using the PSID: 13 survey waves reconciled into a five-wave analysis panel (1999 to 2015).
- **[titanic-survival-analysis](https://github.com/cjcamp16/titanic-survival-analysis)**: EDA and logistic-regression survival modeling on the classic Kaggle dataset.

Also in the works: **Tempo**, an AI scheduling app (FastAPI, Flutter, PostgreSQL, Claude API) with 380+ backend tests. It lives in a private repo for now; the write-up is on my portfolio site.
